Biblical Counselling Worldwide

Where to find a biblical counsellor, and where to train as one

This is a short list of organisations that hold to the sufficiency of Scripture for the care of souls, while welcoming ordinary medical care where it is needed. It lists only a few of the best known and most trusted in each region; inclusion is not endorsement of everything an organisation says, or of every counsellor it lists. Checked November 2026.

Seek a biblical counsellor through your own church first, or through one of these organisations if your church has none, and tell your pastor. A good counsellor will ask you to have a medical check-up, then work through the struggle with you from God’s Word.

Urgent help
Thoughts of suicide, self-harm, psychosis or severe anorexia need medical or emergency help today. Call your local emergency number or see a doctor now; counselling comes alongside that care, never instead of it.

Finding a counsellor

Directories of counsellors who hold to the sufficiency of Scripture. Ask any counsellor about their beliefs, training and church before you begin.

ACBC: Find a Counselor
Association of Certified Biblical Counselors · Kansas City, USA · worldwide directory

The largest certifying body for biblical counsellors. Its map lists certified counsellors and can be searched by place, language, in-person or online, and the counsellor’s sex and areas of help. Most are in North America, with others across the world.

DirectoryCertificationConservative evangelical, broadly Reformed; offices hosted by Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary (Southern Baptist)
CCEF: Find a Biblical Counselor Near You
Christian Counseling & Educational Foundation · Glenside, Pennsylvania, USA

CCEF’s guidance on finding a biblical counsellor where you live. CCEF also has its own team of counsellors, and its writers (David Powlison, Ed Welch, Paul Tripp and others) have shaped biblical counselling for decades.

DirectoryCounsellingReformed evangelical; long ties to Westminster Theological Seminary (Presbyterian)
IABC: Find a Counselor
International Association of Biblical Counselors · USA

Rather than a public map, IABC’s member services office keeps a current list of its counsellors and training centres; you send a short request and they put you in touch.

Referral by requestBroadly evangelical; less confessional than ACBC or CCEF

By region

North America

Faith Biblical Counseling Ministries
Faith Church · Lafayette, Indiana, USA

A church-based counselling centre that sees people in person, and one of the best-known places to train. Its training includes an annual February conference, live-streamed and on-demand tracks (some in Spanish) and a master’s degree through Faith Bible Seminary.

CounsellingTrainingBroadly Reformed evangelical, local-church based
Canadian Biblical Counselling Coalition
Canada · national

A national network founded in 2016 so that every Canadian can reach local biblical care. Its Find a Counsellor page lists partner counsellors by province, with their church and whether they meet in person or online; it also runs an annual conference.

DirectoryConferenceEvangelical, across denominations

United Kingdom and Ireland

Biblical Counselling UK
United Kingdom (including Northern Ireland) · registered charity

Equips local churches to counsel. It runs a certificate programme in partnership with CCEF, with seminars in cities such as London, Edinburgh, Belfast and Sheffield and online. It does not recommend individual counsellors, but offers guidance on finding one, and points people first to their own church’s pastors.

TrainingConservative evangelical, across denominations
ACBC UK
United Kingdom

The UK arm of ACBC, training and certifying biblical counsellors in local churches. Certified counsellors in Britain appear on the ACBC map above.

CertificationAs ACBC: conservative evangelical, broadly Reformed

Continental Europe

Netzwerk Biblische Seelsorge (NBS)
Germany, Austria and Switzerland

The network for biblical soul care in the German-speaking world, founded in 2015 on the model of the Biblical Counseling Coalition. Its partner list names counsellors by town, together with partner churches and training institutions; its yearly Seelsorgekonferenz is well attended.

In GermanPartner listConservative evangelical
EBTC: Biblische Seelsorge
European Bible Training Center · Germany, Switzerland, Austria

A part-time Bible school for church members, whose two-year course in biblical counselling meets on weekends (in 2026/27 in Berlin and Soest). Its teachers include leaders of the German network above, and it publishes German translations of standard biblical counselling books.

In GermanTrainingCalvinistic evangelical; linked to Grace Community Church (John MacArthur)

Africa

ACBC Africa
South Africa · serving the continent

ACBC’s African arm, begun by Wayne Mack, one of the movement’s pioneers, who moved to South Africa. It trains and certifies counsellors for local churches; certified counsellors in Africa appear on the ACBC map.

CertificationReformed evangelical

Latin America and Spanish speakers

ACBC en español
Asociación de Consejeros Bíblicos Certificados · Spanish-speaking world

ACBC’s Spanish site. Certification can be completed wholly in Spanish, through certified training centres that teach in Spanish and with Spanish-speaking supervisors; the ACBC map can be filtered for Spanish-speaking counsellors.

In SpanishCertificationAs ACBC: conservative evangelical, broadly Reformed
Associação Brasileira de Conselheiros Bíblicos (ABCB)
Brazil

The Brazilian association of biblical counsellors, which encourages and sets standards for counselling by pastors, missionaries and church members. It holds a training conference each July in Águas de Lindóia (São Paulo), and lists representative counsellors.

In PortugueseTrainingConservative evangelical, across denominations

Asia and the Pacific

Biblical Counselling Australia
Australia · registered charity

A national network of pastors, counsellors and others, with supervision groups in most capital cities. It publishes a list of accredited biblical counsellors, and its academic training runs through the Presbyterian Theological College in Melbourne, fully online.

Accredited listTrainingEvangelical, across denominations; Presbyterian training partner

Elsewhere in Asia we could not yet confirm a regional body we could recommend with confidence. Use the ACBC map, searching by country or language, and ask your pastor.

Training and resources

For church members, elders and pastors who want to learn to counsel from Scripture. Faith Biblical Counseling Ministries, Biblical Counselling UK, EBTC and ABCB above also train.

CCEF School of Biblical Counseling
Online · from Glenside, Pennsylvania, USA

Ten-week online courses leading to certificates in biblical counselling, with courses on marriage, abuse and counselling in the local church. Some courses count towards Westminster Theological Seminary’s MA in Counseling. CCEF’s articles, books and Journal of Biblical Counseling are a deep well of free and low-cost help.

Online coursesReformed evangelical; long ties to Westminster Theological Seminary (Presbyterian)
IBCD: Care & Discipleship Course
Institute for Biblical Counseling & Discipleship · online · from San Diego, USA

A self-paced video course that many churches use to train members in caring for one another, and which meets ACBC’s first-stage training requirement. Also offered in Spanish.

Online courseEnglish and SpanishReformed evangelical
Biblical Counseling Coalition
USA · international network

A coalition of biblical counselling ministries with a careful confessional statement on what makes counselling biblical. Its site carries a large library of articles, book reviews, a calendar of training events and reports on biblical counselling around the world.

Articles and eventsBroadly evangelical coalition
