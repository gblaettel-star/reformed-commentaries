The Five Solas

The battle cries of the Reformation

The Five Solas were not invented by the Reformers as a marketing slogan. They were hammered out on the anvil of controversy — each one a precise answer to a specific distortion of the Gospel. Together they constitute the Reformation's account of how a guilty sinner stands before a holy God: by Scripture alone, by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, to the glory of God alone.
