Where to Study Theology

Trusted Reformed seminaries, colleges and online courses

This is a short list, not a directory. Each place here teaches confessional Reformed theology, holds Scripture to be inerrant and sufficient, teaches the doctrines of grace and trains people to serve the local church. Presbyterian and Reformed schools (Westminster Standards, Three Forms of Unity) and Reformed Baptist schools (1689) both appear, and each is labelled honestly; where a school differs sharply from this commentary, that is said plainly. Inclusion is not endorsement of everything an institution or its teachers say. The list was checked in November 2026.

A word on baptism: this commentary holds to believers’ baptism (1689 Confession). Presbyterian and Continental Reformed schools baptise the infants of believers. They are honoured brothers in the same Reformed faith, and many Baptist students train with them gladly; simply know where each school stands.

Online and distance

RTS Global
Online, from Charlotte, United States · Presbyterian (Westminster Standards)
The online campus of Reformed Theological Seminary. You can earn a fully online, accredited Master of Arts, or take courses that count towards a residential degree, studying with a local mentor.
Westminster Theological Seminary Online
Online, from Philadelphia, United States · Presbyterian (Westminster Standards)
Westminster’s online programmes include a full Master of Divinity, as well as master’s degrees in religion, counselling and theological studies, taught by the same faculty as the Philadelphia campus.
Reformed Baptist Seminary
Online, United States · Reformed Baptist (1689)
A church-based seminary teaching in harmony with the 1689 Baptist Confession. Its online programmes run from a certificate to the Master of Divinity, with occasional in-person modules hosted by local churches, so students can train without leaving their congregation.
Midwestern Seminary Global Campus
Online, from Kansas City, United States · Southern Baptist (Baptist Faith and Message)
Fully online degrees from bachelor’s to doctorate, with no campus visits required and an emphasis on training “for the church”. Note that its confession is the Baptist Faith and Message, which does not spell out the doctrines of grace as the 1689 Confession does.
Ligonier Connect
Online, from Ligonier Ministries, United States · Reformed
Not a degree, but more than a hundred structured video courses in Bible, theology and church history, with quizzes and discussion questions, by low-cost subscription. Good for personal study or a church group.

North America

Reformed Theological Seminary
Several campuses, United States · Presbyterian (Westminster Standards)
One of the largest Reformed seminaries, with residential campuses in several American cities. It offers the Master of Divinity and a range of master’s and doctoral degrees for pastors, counsellors and lay students.
Westminster Theological Seminary
Philadelphia, United States · Presbyterian (Westminster Standards)
Founded in 1929 to stand for the full authority of Scripture when Princeton drifted. It trains pastors, counsellors and scholars on campus and online.
Westminster Seminary California
Escondido, California, United States · Reformed and Presbyterian (Westminster Standards, Three Forms of Unity)
A small, confessional seminary committed to face-to-face teaching. It offers the Master of Divinity and master’s degrees in biblical, theological and historical studies, and serves both Presbyterian and Continental Reformed churches.
Covenant Theological Seminary
St Louis, Missouri, United States · Presbyterian (Presbyterian Church in America)
The denominational seminary of the Presbyterian Church in America, overseen by its General Assembly. It trains many of that church’s pastors and also offers degrees online.
Greenville Presbyterian Theological Seminary
Greenville, South Carolina, United States · Presbyterian (Westminster Standards)
A seminary with one plain aim: “We train pastors.” It holds firmly to the Westminster Confession, keeps costs low, and makes its programmes available by distance as well as in person.
Puritan Reformed Theological Seminary
Grand Rapids, Michigan, United States · Reformed and Presbyterian (Three Forms of Unity, Westminster Standards)
Known for its love of the Puritans and experiential (heart-searching) Reformed theology. It offers certificates entirely online, a church-embedded Master of Divinity and advanced degrees taught in short modules.
Mid-America Reformed Seminary
Dyer, Indiana, United States · Continental Reformed (Three Forms of Unity, Westminster Standards)
A small seminary in the classic Reformed tradition, focused on preparing ministers through a three-year Master of Divinity, with a shorter master’s degree for those not seeking ordination.
The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary
Louisville, Kentucky, United States · Calvinistic Baptist, Southern Baptist (Abstract of Principles)
Every professor signs the Abstract of Principles (1858), a confession drawn from the 1689 Confession, as well as the Baptist Faith and Message. It offers degrees on campus and many fully online, including several Master of Divinity tracks.
IRBS Theological Seminary
Mansfield, Texas, United States · Reformed Baptist (1689)
Founded as the Institute of Reformed Baptist Studies, now a seminary hosted by a local church. It offers the Master of Divinity, a Master of Arts and a certificate, all grounded in the 1689 Confession.
Reformation Bible College
Sanford, Florida, United States · Reformed (Ligonier Ministries)
An undergraduate college founded by R. C. Sproul, giving a classical, distinctly Reformed education. A good choice for school leavers who want a college degree built on sound theology.

United Kingdom and Ireland

London Seminary
Finchley, London, England · Reformed, independent evangelical
Founded with Martyn Lloyd-Jones to train preachers. Its Pastoral Training Course, for men called to pastor, can be taken full-time, part-time, by module or by livestream from overseas.
Union School of Theology
Bridgend, Wales, and online · Reformed evangelical, interdenominational
Accredited degrees from certificate to doctorate, many of them through online learning communities that let you study from home while serving in your local church.
Edinburgh Theological Seminary
Edinburgh, Scotland, and by distance · Presbyterian (Westminster Standards, Free Church of Scotland)
Reformed training since 1843. Its University of Glasgow degrees (Bachelor and Master of Theology) can be taken full-time, part-time or by distance, and its Saturday courses are open to any church member.
Oak Hill College
Southgate, London, England · Conservative evangelical, Anglican and independent
Trains men and women for ministry in the Church of England and in independent churches, in roughly equal numbers. Its teaching is Reformed in sympathy and firmly evangelical, though it is not bound to a single Reformed confession.

Continental Europe

Theological University Apeldoorn
Apeldoorn, Netherlands · Continental Reformed (Three Forms of Unity)
The university of the Christian Reformed Churches in the Netherlands, teaching theology on a confessional Reformed basis, with Scripture as God’s Word. Most teaching is in Dutch. Note that its churches are currently divided over women in office.

Rest of the world

Mukhanyo Theological College
KwaMhlanga and four other centres, South Africa · Reformed (Three Forms of Unity, Westminster, or 1689)
Equips pastors and other Christians across Africa through registered programmes taught at its main campus and city centres. Its teachers hold to the Reformed confessions, Presbyterian or Baptist.
Queensland Theological College
Brisbane, Australia · Presbyterian (Westminster Confession)
The college of the Presbyterian Church of Queensland, evangelical and Reformed, training pastors and other gospel workers through the Master of Divinity and other awards.
Presbyterian Theological College
Melbourne, Australia, and online · Presbyterian (Westminster Confession)
The college of the Presbyterian Church of Victoria since 1866. It offers awards from certificate to doctorate, and most units can be studied online as well as on campus.
Seminario Reformado Latinoamericano
Latin America and online, in Spanish · Presbyterian (Westminster) or Reformed Baptist (1689)
Spanish-language training from a bachelor’s degree to a doctorate, in person and fully online. Students choose its Presbyterian school or its 1689 Baptist school, according to their own church’s position.

Free online courses

Ligonier teaching series
Online, free · Reformed
R. C. Sproul’s whole library of teaching series is free to stream, together with other free series by teachers such as Sinclair Ferguson and Robert Godfrey. A good first step for anyone.
Third Millennium Ministries (Thirdmill)
Online, free, in several languages · Reformed
A full seminary curriculum on video, audio and text, free to watch or download, in English, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, Russian and more. Thirdmill does not grant degrees, but partner schools can give credit for its courses.
