The Westminster Confession

Of Faith, 1647, in plain English

The Westminster Confession of Faith was written by the Westminster Assembly, more than a hundred ministers and scholars who met in London from 1643 to 1649, and it was completed in 1647. It is the fullest and most careful summary of the Reformed faith in English. The commentaries in this app refer to it often.

Each section is given here in plain, modern English, with a short note on what it means. The Assembly’s own words, with the Bible texts they gave as proof, are folded away under each section. Like every confession, it claims only to say what Scripture says, and it is to be tested by Scripture (1.10).

On baptism, and on a few points about the church and the state, this app holds a different view. Those places are marked Where this app differs, and the Confession’s own text is left as it is.
